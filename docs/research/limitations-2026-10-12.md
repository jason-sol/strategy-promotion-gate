## Definitions

- **OOS Sharpe**: walk-forward select-then-trade Sharpe, the number the gate judges: 'artifact' as recorded, 're-run' recomputed here from today's bars; a row whose two differ by more than 0.01 is footnoted as not reproducible. Annualized with sqrt(252) for every symbol, crypto included
- **SE / 95% CI**: bot.metrics.sharpe_standard_error (Mertens 2002, non-normal returns) on the re-run daily series; CI = re-run Sharpe +/- 1.96 SE
- **Variant OOS**: the validated variant's own concatenated OOS Sharpe (artifact); differs from the gate number whenever fold selection changed hands
- **DSR**: the validated variant's Deflated Sharpe Ratio (artifact)
- **OOS trades**: closed round-trips on the select-then-trade path: each fold's in-sample winner re-run over its test window; a position still open at a fold's end is not counted
- **Gate trials**: shortlist size the DSR deflated against (len(candidates))
- **Search trials**: configurations tried in a recorded search that produced the shortlist; 'not recorded' means no search file exists, not zero
- **Live slippage**: bot.portfolio.slippage_summary over the fills log; below 20 measured fills per symbol it is reported as a sample size only
